Work About Resume

Senior UX Designer with 8+ years of experience helping complex products become clearer, more inclusive, and easier to build, with AI as a design partner.

Hi, I’m Amandine Revol. Senior Product Designer at JobCloud, Switzerland’s leading digital job platform. I work across product strategy, UX design, design systems, and cross-functional collaboration, with a particular interest in accessibility, UX writing, and practical applications of AI.

In 2025, I led the UX work for the brand refresh of jobs.ch and jobup.ch, a year-long project involving brand, UX, and engineering. I led a team of four designers and helped translate the new brand into scalable product experiences across web and native apps, reaching more than 6 million monthly visitors combined.

With a fixed launch deadline, I partnered with engineering and stakeholders to set priorities, resolve dependencies, and coordinate the UX work required to deliver the refreshed experience on time across platforms.

I also co-lead JobCloud’s design system, working with designers and developers to improve consistency, scalability, and usability across products. Alongside my product work, I’m exploring how AI can make both job seeking and design workflows smarter.

Before joining JobCloud, I designed products at Levuro, a Swiss startup focused on social media publishing and livestreaming, and at Marinus Analytics in Pittsburgh, USA, where I worked on software supporting law enforcement investigations into human trafficking. Earlier in my career, I spent four years designing e-commerce experiences at Bubble-Diving in France.

I’m based in Switzerland and share my home office with Anja, my rescue dog and unofficial design-review participant.
